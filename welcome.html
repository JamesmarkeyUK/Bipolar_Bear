Share your thoughts,
not your identity.

A separate, standalone app: an anonymous peer community for people living with bipolar. Pick a monika, post what’s real, and find people who actually get it.

  • ✓ No real name — just a monika you choose
  • ✓ Posts to relate to, not perform for
  • ✓ Recognised across all your devices
  • ✓ Your email is only ever stored as a hash
